About NOVA Terra

Saudi-Based. Built to Move the Kingdom.

Founded on discipline, precision, and Vision 2030 alignment — we are the ground force behind Saudi Arabia's most ambitious construction and infrastructure projects.

Our Story
"Built from the ground up in Riyadh — one truck, one contract, one promise kept at a time."

NOVA Terra was founded in Riyadh by heavy transport veterans who saw a gap in the market: Saudi Arabia's construction boom demanded a fleet operator that could match the pace, scale, and exacting standards of giga-project contractors. We started with a small fleet of dump trucks and a single regional contract — and earned every expansion through flawless execution.

From a regional contractor serving Riyadh's construction corridors, we grew systematically into a national fleet operator with coverage across all 12 KSA administrative regions. Each step of that growth was funded by performance, not shortcuts — we never scaled faster than our ability to maintain quality, safety, and reliability.

Today, NOVA Terra is ISO-certified, Vision 2030 aligned, and operates a fleet of 150+ vehicles ranging from standard dump trucks to articulated off-road units and long-haul tipper trains. We are a trusted partner to some of Saudi Arabia's most demanding project developers — and we intend to keep it that way.
