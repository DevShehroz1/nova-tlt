About Us

Saudi Arabia's leading heavy haulage and earthmoving specialist, built for Vision 2030 and beyond — moving the Kingdom's most critical projects since 2004.

Built on two decades of precision and trust, Nova Terra moves Saudi Arabia's most critical infrastructure with 150 heavy vehicles delivering proven excellence across every region of the Kingdom.

Our Story

Built From the Ground Up

NOVA Terra was established in 2004 with a modest fleet of 12 dump trucks and a single mission: to move Saudi Arabia's most demanding projects on time and on spec. Over the following two decades, we grew alongside the Kingdom — from the early days of industrial city development to today's giga-project era.

Today, our 150-vehicle fleet operates across all 12 KSA regions, serving the Kingdom's largest construction contractors, mining operators, government agencies, and Vision 2030 flagship projects. Every truck in our fleet is GPS-tracked, fully licensed, and operated by certified, HSE-trained professionals.
