Safety Framework

Three Pillars.
Zero Incidents.

🧠

Prevention

  • Pre-job safety briefings before every shift and site mobilisation
  • Route hazard assessments for all new site approaches
  • Load weight verification against vehicle GVM limits
  • Mandatory pre-departure vehicle inspection checklists
  • Speed limit and geofence enforcement via telematics
⚡

Response

  • 24/7 incident hotline staffed by trained safety personnel
  • Emergency response protocol activated within 15 minutes
  • On-site first aid kits and fire extinguishers on every vehicle
  • Driver emergency shutdown training — annual re-certification
  • Client HSE team notification within 30 minutes of any event
📊

Reporting

  • Every near-miss logged, investigated, and closed out
  • Weekly safety performance reviews with fleet management
  • Transparent KPI reporting shared with contract clients
  • Monthly safety bulletin distributed to all drivers
  • Annual third-party HSE audit with published results

Driver Training

40 Hours a Year.
No Exceptions.

Every NOVA Terra operator undergoes mandatory annual recertification. Our training program goes beyond the GDT licence minimum — combining defensive driving science, emergency protocols, and site-specific HSE requirements into a comprehensive 40-hour curriculum.

All drivers hold valid Saudi General Directorate of Traffic heavy vehicle licences. Foreign operators are additionally screened for equivalency before joining the fleet.

1

40-Hour Annual Recertification

Full classroom and practical recertification completed every 12 months — no partial credits, no deferrals.

2

Defensive Driving Course

Fatigue management, adverse weather driving, blind-spot awareness, and high-load braking distance training.

3

Emergency Response & First Aid

Basic life support, vehicle fire response, rollover protocol, and communication procedures under Saudi Red Crescent guidance.

4

Load Securing & HSE Site Protocols

Correct load distribution, tarp and tailgate procedures, and site-specific induction requirements for construction and mining environments.

5

Saudi GDT Licensed Operators Only

Every driver holds a valid KSA heavy vehicle licence. All credentials are verified and recorded in the NOVA Terra driver registry before first deployment.
