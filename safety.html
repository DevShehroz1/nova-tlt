Safety Pillars

Three Pillars. Zero Compromise.

🧠

Prevention

Safety starts before the key turns. Every NOVA Terra project begins with a formal risk assessment and hazard identification process.

  • Site-specific risk assessment
  • Hazard identification and control
  • Pre-mobilisation safety briefing
  • Driver fitness assessment
  • Route survey and clearance checks
⚡

Response

When incidents occur, our response is immediate — first aid trained operators, on-site emergency procedures, and 24/7 operations centre escalation.

  • First aid trained operators on every shift
  • Emergency response plan per project
  • 24/7 operations centre escalation
  • Designated site emergency coordinator
  • Liaison with local emergency services
📊

Reporting

Transparent reporting is central to our safety culture — every near-miss is documented, investigated, and used to improve our systems.

  • Near-miss reporting system
  • Monthly HSE performance reports
  • Client-accessible incident log
  • Root cause analysis for all incidents
  • Annual HSE performance review

Driver Training

Every Driver Certified. Every Time.

No operator drives a NOVA Terra vehicle without completing our full 40-hour induction programme — plus annual refresher training and site-specific briefings on every new project.

01
Induction & Orientation
20-hour foundation programme covering NOVA Terra policies, Saudi road regulations, and general heavy vehicle safety protocols.
02
Vehicle-Specific Training
Hands-on familiarisation with the assigned vehicle class — controls, load limits, tipping operations, and emergency procedures specific to that vehicle type.
03
HSE Certification
HSE certification examination — operators must achieve a minimum 80% pass mark before they are cleared for independent operation.
04
Site Briefing
Project-specific induction covering site hazards, access routes, emergency assembly points, and client-specific safety requirements.
05
Annual Refresher
Mandatory annual refresher training — including updated regulations, incident lessons learned, and defensive driving assessment.
