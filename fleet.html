Maintenance Standards

Fleet Kept at
Peak Condition

Every NOVA Terra vehicle follows a rigorous preventive maintenance schedule — ensuring reliability, safety, and minimal downtime on your project.

Preventive Maintenance

All vehicles follow manufacturer-recommended service intervals, with full records maintained in our fleet management system.

Daily Pre-Trip Inspections

Operators complete a structured 20-point inspection before every shift. Non-compliant units are pulled from service immediately.

GPS & Telematics

100% of fleet equipped with real-time GPS tracking and engine telematics for visibility, efficiency, and compliance reporting.

On-Site Workshop Support

Mobile maintenance units and on-site mechanics available for large fleet contracts to minimise response times and keep operations moving.

4-Hour Replacement Guarantee

If a unit goes down during your contract, a replacement is dispatched within 4 hours — no project delays, no exceptions.

Compliance Documentation

Full registration, insurance, operator licensing, and inspection documentation available at all times for client and regulatory review.
