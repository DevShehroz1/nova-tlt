Built From the Ground Up
NOVA Terra was established in 2022 with a clear mission: to move Saudi Arabia's most demanding projects on time and on spec. Built on 5 years of collective expertise in heavy logistics, fleet operations, and HSE, we have grown rapidly alongside the Kingdom's giga-project era.
Today, our 50+ vehicle fleet operates across KSA's most active regions, serving construction contractors, mining operators, government agencies, and Vision 2030 flagship projects. Every truck in our fleet is GPS-tracked, fully licensed, and operated by certified, HSE-trained professionals.
