Introduction

Saudi-Based.
Built to Move
the Kingdom.

NOVA Terra Logistics & Transport is a Saudi-based heavy equipment and transport company delivering reliable dump truck rental, earthmoving, and logistics services to the Kingdom's most demanding sectors.

Built on discipline, precision, and Vision 2030-aligned growth, we move the materials that build the nation — from giga-projects to regional infrastructure, we're the ground force behind the Kingdom's progress.
