NOVA Terra Expands Fleet with 20 New Volvo FMX Units

NOVA Terra has completed the induction of 20 new Volvo FMX heavy-duty dump trucks, increasing our total fleet capacity to over 150 vehicles. The expansion supports growing demand from construction and giga-project contractors across the Kingdom, with the new units deployed immediately to active NEOM and Riyadh infrastructure assignments. Each vehicle is fitted with full telematics, fatigue monitoring, and blind-spot cameras as standard.
