Our Process

How We Handle
Every Project

01
📋

Planning

We assess your site, scope, and schedule before a single truck moves. Route surveys, load calculations, and HSE documentation are completed in advance.

  • Site visit and route hazard assessment
  • Fleet sizing and category recommendation
  • Project timeline and milestone mapping
  • HSE compliance documentation preparation
02
🚛

Deployment

Trucks are pre-inspected, crews are briefed, and dispatch is coordinated to your project start date with a dedicated site supervisor for the first mobilisation.

  • Pre-departure vehicle inspection sign-off
  • Driver briefing with site-specific HSE rules
  • GPS tracking activated and client portal access granted
  • Dedicated fleet coordinator assigned to your project
03
✅

Delivery

Daily load reports, weekly progress summaries, and transparent billing ensure you always know what's moving and what it costs — no surprises at month end.

  • Daily tonnage and trip count reporting
  • Weekly client progress review calls
  • Transparent itemised invoicing
  • Post-project HSE and performance summary
