FAQs

Common Questions

What is the minimum rental period?

For flexible daily rental, the minimum period is a single calendar day. There is no multi-day minimum. For project-based contracts the minimum engagement is three months, which is when the fixed monthly rate and dedicated fleet allocation apply.

Do your trucks come with operators?

Operators are optional on all standard and long-haul rental units. For heavy-duty and articulated dump trucks we require our certified operators for safety and insurance compliance. All NOVA Terra operators hold Saudi General Directorate of Traffic licenses and complete annual HSE re-certification.

Can I scale my fleet mid-project?

Yes. On daily rental agreements you can add or remove trucks with 24 hours' notice. On project contracts, additions can be mobilised within 48 hours. Reductions require 7 days' written notice so we can reposition assets without disrupting your operations.

What regions do you cover?

NOVA Terra operates across all 12 administrative regions of Saudi Arabia, with primary depots in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and NEOM's Tabuk corridor. Remote region deployments are available with a 24-hour lead time. Cross-region long-haul transport is a standard service offering.

What happens if a truck breaks down on site?

We guarantee a replacement vehicle on site within 4 hours for any in-region breakdown. Our spare fleet reserve is maintained specifically for this purpose. You are not billed for any period the original unit is out of service, and the replacement is provided at no additional mobilisation cost.
