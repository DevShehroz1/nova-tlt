The Process

From Call to On-Site

Three steps from your first contact to full fleet deployment.

01

Requirements

Share your project scope, timeline, site location, and material type. Our operations team assesses your needs within 2 hours and recommends the optimal fleet configuration.

02

Fleet Plan

We prepare a detailed fleet plan with truck count, operator allocation, shift schedule, and maintenance intervals — along with a fixed-price quote valid for 7 days.

03

Sign the contract and we deploy. Same-day dispatch for standard rentals, 48-hour mobilisation for full project contracts. Your site coordinator gets direct contact with our dispatch team.
